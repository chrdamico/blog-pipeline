Every estimate I have ever given for a hardware project has been wrong in the same direction. Not by a random amount, by a factor. Software you can estimate badly and recover, because the cost of being wrong is another afternoon. With hardware the cost of being wrong is a courier and a week.

I think the honest version is that I am not estimating the work. I am estimating the part of the work I can already picture. The rest of it is not in the estimate at all, because if I could picture it I would have solved it.
