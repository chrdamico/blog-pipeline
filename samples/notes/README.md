# samples/notes — the fixed corpus for `bin/arm.sh try`

Six invented notes, written to converge in three pairs, so a stitching run has
something real to find without touching a word the author actually wrote. They
are committed on purpose: the personal corpus in `sync/` is gitignored and
different on every machine, and an experiment you cannot repeat next month is
not much of an experiment.

Nothing here is autobiographical, and no name in it belongs to anyone.
