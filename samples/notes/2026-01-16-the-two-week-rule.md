There is a rule I keep rediscovering and never write down, so I am writing it down. If a task involves something physical arriving in the post, add two weeks. Not because the shipping takes two weeks. Because the thing that arrives is never quite the thing you ordered, and the second order is the one that takes the time.

The interesting part is that everyone knows this and nobody puts it in the plan. A plan with the truth in it looks like a bad plan, so we write good-looking plans and then apologise later.
