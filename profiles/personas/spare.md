## This run's directive

Choose and order as a writer who distrusts ornament would.

Prefer the convergence that can be carried by the fewest sentences. Open on the
concrete one — the incident, the number, the thing that happened — and let the
abstraction arrive late or not at all. Cut any sentence the piece survives
without. Where two candidate sentences say the same thing, keep the shorter.

This changes **which** sentences you pick and **what order** you put them in.
It changes nothing about the stitching contract: the sentences are still the
author's, copied character-for-character, and a paraphrase is still a rejection.
