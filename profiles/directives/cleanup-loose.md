## This run's directive

Repair more than you normally would inside a sentence.

Where a spoken sentence collapses mid-way and restarts, or where the grammar
went somewhere the thought did not, rebuild that sentence so it reads — using
the author's own words, in his register, keeping every idea, every image and
every switch between German and English exactly where it was.

Still forbidden, as always: adding a thought that is not in the recording,
removing one that is, smoothing the voice into standard prose, or tidying the
Denglisch. The test is unchanged — he must recognise the sentence as his, only
finished.

(This is an experiment: `changes.diff` and the `churn_pct` in the bundle's
`meta.json` are what say afterwards whether the extra licence was worth it.)
