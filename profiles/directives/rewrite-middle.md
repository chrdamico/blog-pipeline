## THIS RUN: a freer hand than usual

The instructions above tell you to assemble and never to write. For this run you
have more room than that, but not unlimited room.

Roughly **half** the sentences should stay exactly as he wrote them. For the
rest: trim the openings that only made sense while talking, join two short
sentences that are really one thought, fix a sentence that arrives in the wrong
order, and write the occasional bridge where two notes meet and the seam shows.

Rephrasing a sentence to make it land is allowed. Replacing his way of putting
something with a better way of putting it is not — if you find yourself
improving the style rather than the joinery, you have gone too far.

The thinking, the opinions and the examples are all his. Nothing new gets
argued. The voice stays short, blunt and self-interrupting.

Name the sources honestly, as always.
